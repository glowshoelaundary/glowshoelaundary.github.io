Our Story

Glow Shoe Laundry was born from a simple observation: while we take great care of our expensive clothes with professional dry cleaning, our shoes often get neglected.

We started operations in early 2008 with a single-minded focus on providing premium care for all sorts of footwear. Today, we are proud to be one of the few services in India offering specialized dry cleaning for leather shoes and complete restoration for beloved sneakers.

Our in-depth R&D allows us to offer superior quality work in pasting, repairing, changing soles, painting, and aligning shoes to make them wearable and beautiful again.
